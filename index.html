Welcome to the ultimate IVE fan site! IVE is a 6-member South Korean girl group under Starship Entertainment, debuted on December 1, 2021, with their hit single "ELEVEN". Known for their unique concept of "self-confidence" and "girls who have everything," IVE has taken the K-pop world by storm with their catchy melodies, stunning visuals, and powerful performances.

IVE's Remarkable Journey

IVE has shown consistent growth since their debut in 2021, with sales peaking in 2022-2023 and maintaining strong performance through 2025. Their ability to consistently release hit albums while maintaining artistic integrity has established them as one of K-pop's leading girl groups. The group's unique concept and relatable lyrics have resonated with fans worldwide, resulting in over 12 million albums sold globally.

Key milestones include their breakthrough with "Love Dive" in 2022, the million-seller achievement with "After LIKE," and their continued dominance in 2025 with multiple chart-topping releases including "IVE EMPATHY" and "IVE SECRET."
